# Editing rules for this site

Personal academic website and CV for Will Hardy, built with Quarto and published
by GitHub Actions (see `docs/adr/`). Terms are defined in `GLOSSARY.md`.

## Content constraints

- **Research consulting** is listed accurately on the CV (2018–present, with
  clients and reports). Elsewhere on the site, do not promote it: no Consulting
  page, no client names outside the CV, no "work with me" calls to action. Will's
  employer does not employ him to consult, and he does not want to raise its
  profile.
- Publications are listed only with a status defined in `GLOSSARY.md`; never add
  work "in preparation".
- Affiliations in publication records stay as they were at the time of
  publication.
